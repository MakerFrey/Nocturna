# Nocturna — Design Review Slides

## Overview

This folder contains the design review presentations created throughout the development of **Nocturna**, an autonomous quadrupedal robotic system designed for nighttime wildlife monitoring, environmental mapping, and wildlife activity analysis.

These presentations document the team's engineering contributions, technical progress, design decisions, challenges, and proposed solutions throughout the senior design project.

## Current Contents

### Initial Design Review

The **Initial Design Review** presentation documents the current development status of Nocturna during the Fall 2026 semester.

Each team member presents their individual technical contributions through three primary sections:

1. **Project Contribution**
   - Individual engineering responsibilities and major deliverables.
   - High-level system architecture highlighting assigned subsystems.
   - Low-level diagrams illustrating specific engineering contributions.

2. **Work Completed**
   - Planned and completed engineering tasks.
   - Current development progress.
   - Estimated completion percentages for individual tasks.

3. **Current Obstacles & Next Steps**
   - Technical challenges encountered during development.
   - Proposed engineering solutions.
   - Remaining tasks and anticipated completion dates.
